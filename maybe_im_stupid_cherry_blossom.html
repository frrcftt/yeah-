Maybe I'm
stupid.

some feelings are difficult to name
♡

Yes I'm stupid... maybe more than you think. I'm stupid enough to feel things deeply even when I pretend nothing matters. I get attached to little things, remember words people forget, and find meaning in moments that probably meant nothing to anyone else. I overthink, I care silently, I hide what hurts me behind a smile, and sometimes I act like I don't need anyone when deep down I just wish someone would understand me without having to explain everything. I know I make mistakes, I trust too easily, and sometimes I give people more importance than they deserve. Maybe that's what makes me stupid. I have a thousand thoughts in my mind but barely know how to express even one of them. I can laugh with everyone and still feel like nobody really knows what's going on inside me. So yes, I'm stupid... not because I don't understand the world, but because even after everything, I still feel things that I wish I could simply stop caring about.

♥ some feelings are hard to explain ♥
made of petals & unspoken thoughts
